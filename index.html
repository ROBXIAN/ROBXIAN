Innovation has no limits.

Robotics combines engineering, science and computing to create machines that help people in everyday life.

About Robotics

Robotics is a field of technology concerned with designing, building, programming and operating robots.

⚙️

Engineering

Engineers design the physical structure and moving parts of robots.

💻

Programming

Computer programs give robots instructions to follow.

📡

Sensors

Sensors help robots detect information about their surroundings.

Why is robotics important?

Robots can perform repetitive tasks, assist people, work in difficult environments and improve accuracy in many industries.

The History of Robots

Robotics developed over many centuries, from early mechanical inventions to modern intelligent machines.

Ancient Times

Inventors created mechanical devices that could perform simple movements.

1920

The word "robot" became popular through the play R.U.R. by Karel Čapek.

1950s

George Devol developed an early programmable industrial robot.

Today

Robots are used in factories, hospitals, homes, research and space exploration.

A remarkable journey

Robotics continues to develop as engineers create more capable and useful machines.

Types of Robots

Robots come in many shapes and sizes. Each type is designed for different tasks.

🏭

Industrial Robots

Used in factories for manufacturing and assembly.

🏥

Medical Robots

Assist medical professionals with certain procedures and tasks.

🚀

Space Robots

Explore planets and collect scientific information.

🏠

Domestic Robots

Help with household activities such as cleaning.

🐕

Humanoid Robots

Designed with features or movements resembling humans.

🌊

Underwater Robots

Explore underwater environments and collect data.

Industrial Robotics

Industrial robots are machines used in manufacturing facilities to perform tasks with speed, consistency and precision.

🚗

Car Manufacturing

Robotic arms help assemble vehicles and move components.

📦

Packaging

Robots sort, pack and organise products.

🔧

Assembly

Machines help put together products in factories.

Did you know?

Some industrial robots can repeat the same programmed movement thousands of times.

Medical Robots

Medical robotics supports healthcare professionals in selected medical procedures, rehabilitation and hospital services.

🩺

Surgical Assistance

Some robotic systems help trained surgeons control specialised instruments.

🦾

Rehabilitation

Robotic devices can support physical rehabilitation exercises.

💊

Hospital Services

Robots can help transport supplies and medicines in hospitals.

People remain essential.

Medical robots are tools. Healthcare professionals make important decisions and supervise patient care.

Robots in Space

Space robots help scientists investigate places that are difficult or dangerous for humans to reach.

🔴

Mars Rovers

Robotic vehicles explore the surface of Mars and study rocks and soil.

🛰️

Satellites

Robotic spacecraft collect information about Earth and space.

🌕

Lunar Exploration

Robotic missions help scientists study the Moon.

Famous example: Perseverance

NASA's Perseverance rover landed on Mars in February 2021 to explore the planet and investigate its ancient environment.

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computing that develops systems able to perform tasks that normally require human-like abilities, such as recognising patterns and understanding language.

🧠

Machine Learning

Computers learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions.

💬

Language Systems

AI can process and generate human language.

👁️

Computer Vision

AI systems can analyse images and identify objects.

AI and Robotics

AI can help some robots understand information and respond to their surroundings. Not every robot uses AI.
